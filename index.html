📖 Help & Complete Setup Guide

1. How to Process Matches

  • Fetch or Paste Payload: Click "Fetch Current Week's Matches" (pulls from Listener Sheet via Col I) or paste a raw score payload in Tab 1.

  • Send to Main Sheet: Sends payload to Main Sheet (Col J: approved matches).
